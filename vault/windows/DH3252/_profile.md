## Tips

- Drain slots on the bottom face OUT - confirm before lifting it in.
- Flash the sides first, top flashing last over the flange lip.
- Caulk flanges left/right/top, never the bottom - the bottom has to drain.
- Level first, then center. Set the flange screws, then remove the shims.
- Check both sashes slide before you screw off - a racked frame binds a double-hung fast.

## Watch-outs

- Do not seal the bottom weep holes - a double-hung will pond water if you do.
- Confirm stucco vs rock setout before install.
- On hot days ease off screw torque on vinyl frames.
