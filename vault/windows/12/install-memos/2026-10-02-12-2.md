## Safety notes

Adding simple wooden cleats on an inclined roof is an incredibly simple and effective way to make installs safer when on a roof without tie off gear. Its always worth doing in lieu of any better safety measures, it can also double as a shelf to hold your tools and materials so they don't slide off and hit someone, and you dont have to waste time retrieving them or put yourself at risk by trying work while holding a clumsy armload of stuff

## What we'd do again next time

Exactly what we did. Plan ahead, take safety measures, and have a couple extra men helping with the window while maneuvering it on awkward footing so that one guy slipping slightly doesn't turn into both men falling off the roof with a heavy window
