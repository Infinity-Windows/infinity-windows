## Tips

- Find the drain slots on the bottom of the frame - that side faces OUT. Never set it drain-side in.
- Flash both sides before you set the window; roll the TOP flashing last, over the flange lip, so water sheds down.
- Caulk the flanges left, right and top only - leave the bottom open so trapped water can drain.
- Shim and level first, center second. Level always wins. Set screws through the flanges, then pull the shims.
- St. George standard: caulk off the top and side weep holes, leave the bottom drains open.

## Watch-outs

- Out-of-level wall: shim to true level even if the reveal looks uneven - flag the foreman if it is more than ~1 in off.
- Confirm stucco vs rock before setting depth - it changes the setout ~1 in (rock) vs ~1.5 in (stucco).
- Vinyl softens in high heat - do not overdrive screws or force a bind on hot days.
