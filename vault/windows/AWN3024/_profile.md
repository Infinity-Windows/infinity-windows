## Tips

- Drain side out; flash sides first, top flashing last over the flange.
- Level matters - an awning that is off will not seal against the frame when cranked shut.
- Caulk flanges left/right/top only; leave the bottom to drain.
- Set flange screws after leveling, pull shims, then crank through full travel.
- Confirm the arm hardware operates smoothly before final caulk.

## Watch-outs

- An out-of-level awning leaks at the hinge side - level carefully.
- Confirm setout depth up front.
- Do not force vinyl on hot days.
