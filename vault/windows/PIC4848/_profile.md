## Tips

- Stationary unit - no moving parts, so the finish caulk is the whole quality story.
- Drain side out; flash sides first, top flashing last over the flange lip.
- Level and center - with no sash to hide it, an out-of-level picture window shows.
- Exterior finish caulk is permanent: tape a ~3/4 in gap, caulk, then tool one smooth line.
- Photograph the finished caulk line and that all tape/stickers are removed.

## Watch-outs

- Sloppy tooling on the exterior caulk is the most common callback on picture windows.
- Confirm setout depth (stucco vs rock).
- Big glass = 2-person lift; keep clear of the drop zone.
