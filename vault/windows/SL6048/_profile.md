## Tips

- This is a 2-person lift - use suction cups or straps. Set the bottom into the frame, then tilt up.
- Get it dead level before screwing off or the panels will not roll smoothly.
- Drain side out; flash sides first, top flashing last over the flange lip.
- Caulk flanges left/right/top, leave the bottom to drain.
- Roll both panels full travel before final caulk to confirm no bind.

## Watch-outs

- A racked slider binds - level and square matter more here than on a small window.
- Never get under a suspended panel during the lift.
- Confirm stucco vs rock setout before setting depth.
