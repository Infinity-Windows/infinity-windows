# Watch-outs & pitfalls (all types)

_Generated from Supabase by scripts/vault-sync.mjs — do not edit by hand._

## Awning 30x24 (AWN3024)

- An out-of-level awning leaks at the hinge side - level carefully.
- Confirm setout depth up front.
- Do not force vinyl on hot days.

## Bay 72x48 (BAY7248)

- Bays fail from under-support and roof-junction leaks - never skip the bracing or seat flashing.
- 3-person job with heavy glass - stage the lift and keep the drop zone clear.
- Confirm the opening framing and setout with the foreman before starting - bays are non-starters if the rough opening is wrong.

## Casement 30x48 (CAS3048)

- A casement that is not dead level will not latch - do not rush the level step.
- Confirm setout depth (stucco vs rock) up front.
- Watch the sash edge for scratches when unpackaging with a utility knife.

## Casement 30x50 (CAS3050)

- Upper hinge screws can cam out on casements
- Out-of-square RO by 3/8" will blow the time estimate
- Tempered edges — watch fingers

## Double-Hung 32x52 (DH3252)

- Do not seal the bottom weep holes - a double-hung will pond water if you do.
- Confirm stucco vs rock setout before install.
- On hot days ease off screw torque on vinyl frames.

## Hopper 30x24 (HOP3024)

- Common in basements/wells - verify the well drains so the unit does not sit in water.
- Confirm setout depth (stucco vs rock).
- Do not seal the bottom weep holes.

## Picture 48x48 (PIC4848)

- Sloppy tooling on the exterior caulk is the most common callback on picture windows.
- Confirm setout depth (stucco vs rock).
- Big glass = 2-person lift; keep clear of the drop zone.

## Picture 60x48 (PIC6048)

- Tooling quality varies by installer - a bumpy caulk line is a visible defect.
- Confirm stucco vs rock setout.
- Keep clear of suspended glass during the lift.

## Single-Hung 32x52 (SH3252)

- Out-of-level wall: shim to true level even if the reveal looks uneven - flag the foreman if it is more than ~1 in off.
- Confirm stucco vs rock before setting depth - it changes the setout ~1 in (rock) vs ~1.5 in (stucco).
- Vinyl softens in high heat - do not overdrive screws or force a bind on hot days.

## Slider 60x48 (SL6048)

- A racked slider binds - level and square matter more here than on a small window.
- Never get under a suspended panel during the lift.
- Confirm stucco vs rock setout before setting depth.

## Slider 72x48 (SL7248)

- Heavy glass - keep everyone clear of the drop zone and the suction path.
- A slightly racked large slider will not seal or roll - do not accept it.
- Confirm the suction-cup machine is on site before scheduling this unit.
