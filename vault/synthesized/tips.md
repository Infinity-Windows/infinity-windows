# Install tips (all types)

_Generated from Supabase by scripts/vault-sync.mjs — do not edit by hand._

## Awning 30x24 (AWN3024)

- Drain side out; flash sides first, top flashing last over the flange.
- Level matters - an awning that is off will not seal against the frame when cranked shut.
- Caulk flanges left/right/top only; leave the bottom to drain.
- Set flange screws after leveling, pull shims, then crank through full travel.
- Confirm the arm hardware operates smoothly before final caulk.

## Bay 72x48 (BAY7248)

- Bay is a structural, 3-person unit - it needs support below and cable/knee bracing above per plan.
- Confirm the head board and support brackets are in before you hang any weight.
- Flash the full perimeter carefully; the roof/seat connections are the leak risk, not just the flanges.
- Level and center the center lite first, then bring the flankers to it.
- Seal the seat and roof junctions and photograph every flashing/caulk joint.

## Casement 30x48 (CAS3048)

- Drain side faces OUT. Flash sides before setting, top flashing last over the flange.
- Level is critical on a casement - if it is out, the sash will not seal or will swing on its own.
- Caulk flanges left/right/top only; leave the bottom for drainage.
- Set screws through the flange after leveling, then pull the shims and check the sash swings clean.
- Test the crank through full travel before you finish caulk.

## Casement 30x50 (CAS3050)

- Pre-check RO with diagonal tape before setting the unit
- Stage the unit outside the opening before the lift
- Use impact with clutch + plastic shims on casement hinges
- Bring a Japanese pull saw when the RO is racked
- Call out pinch points on the swing path

## Double-Hung 32x52 (DH3252)

- Drain slots on the bottom face OUT - confirm before lifting it in.
- Flash the sides first, top flashing last over the flange lip.
- Caulk flanges left/right/top, never the bottom - the bottom has to drain.
- Level first, then center. Set the flange screws, then remove the shims.
- Check both sashes slide before you screw off - a racked frame binds a double-hung fast.

## Hopper 30x24 (HOP3024)

- Hopper opens inward at the top - confirm swing clearance inside before setting.
- Drain side out; flash sides first, top flashing last over the flange lip.
- Level first - a racked hopper will not seal at the sill.
- Caulk flanges left/right/top, leave the bottom to drain.
- Test the latch and full open/close before final caulk.

## Picture 48x48 (PIC4848)

- Stationary unit - no moving parts, so the finish caulk is the whole quality story.
- Drain side out; flash sides first, top flashing last over the flange lip.
- Level and center - with no sash to hide it, an out-of-level picture window shows.
- Exterior finish caulk is permanent: tape a ~3/4 in gap, caulk, then tool one smooth line.
- Photograph the finished caulk line and that all tape/stickers are removed.

## Picture 60x48 (PIC6048)

- Stationary - the exterior caulk finish is the whole job quality; take your time tooling it.
- 2-person lift; set the bottom in, tilt up, drain side facing out.
- Flash sides first, top flashing last over the flange lip.
- Level and center; caulk flanges left/right/top, leave the bottom to drain.
- Photograph the final caulk line and confirm tape/stickers removed.

## Single-Hung 32x52 (SH3252)

- Find the drain slots on the bottom of the frame - that side faces OUT. Never set it drain-side in.
- Flash both sides before you set the window; roll the TOP flashing last, over the flange lip, so water sheds down.
- Caulk the flanges left, right and top only - leave the bottom open so trapped water can drain.
- Shim and level first, center second. Level always wins. Set screws through the flanges, then pull the shims.
- St. George standard: caulk off the top and side weep holes, leave the bottom drains open.

## Slider 60x48 (SL6048)

- This is a 2-person lift - use suction cups or straps. Set the bottom into the frame, then tilt up.
- Get it dead level before screwing off or the panels will not roll smoothly.
- Drain side out; flash sides first, top flashing last over the flange lip.
- Caulk flanges left/right/top, leave the bottom to drain.
- Roll both panels full travel before final caulk to confirm no bind.

## Slider 72x48 (SL7248)

- Large slider - plan a 2-person lift with the suction machine; never solo it.
- Level and square are everything - test-roll the panels before you commit screws.
- Drain out; flash the sides, then top flashing last over the flange.
- Shim to level, set flange screws, pull shims, re-check the roll.
- Caulk flanges left/right/top only; leave the bottom open.
